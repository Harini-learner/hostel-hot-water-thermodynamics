# Field Observation & Documentary Execution Plan

## 1. Executive Overview & Scope
This plan details the technical documentary framework and field observation protocol for the campus hostel water heating audit. The visual evidence documents the operational mechanics, thermodynamic performance, standby losses, and piping dynamics of 58 electric storage water heaters deployed across seven girl hostel blocks.

---

## 2. Production & Scene Structure

**Scene 1: Campus Infrastructure Overview**
* **Visual Focus:** Exterior wide shots of the seven hostel blocks constituting the decentralized water heating network.
* **Narrative Track:** Introduces the distributed heating architecture, contrasting localized point-of-use units against centralized boiler systems in terms of installation simplicity and thermal distribution efficiency.

**Scene 2: Representative Equipment Installations**
* **Visual Focus:** Physical layout of wall-mounted storage units (V-Guard Steamer Plus and Venus Magma Plus series), high-temperature Ashirvad FlowGuard Plus CPVC piping, flexible stainless steel braided hoses, and dual-tap discharge manifolds.
* **Narrative Track:** Demonstrates standard mounting heights, isolation valve accessibility, and plumbing integration within common washroom facilities.

**Scene 3: Nameplate & Technical Evidence Verification**
* **Visual Focus:** Close-up inspection of factory-stamped metallic nameplates and BEE Energy Rating labels.
* **Narrative Track:** Verifies system rating parameters:
  * Nominal Volume: 25 Liters
  * Heating Capacity: 2.0 kW (2000 W) at 230 V AC, 50 Hz
  * Maximum Operating Pressure: 0.8 MPa (8 bar)
  * BEE Verified Standing Loss: 0.511 kWh/24hr

**Scene 4: Thermal Measurement & Discharge Baseline**
* **Visual Focus:** Calibrated digital thermometer readings taken at supply inlet and tap outlet during active draw.
* **Narrative Track:** Establishes thermal elevation:
  * Ambient / Cold Supply Temperature: 37 °C
  * Heated Discharge Temperature: Exceeding 45 °C (up to 65 °C at full cutoff)
  * Calculated Differential: ΔT = 8 °C (standard batch calculation reference)

**Scene 5: Purge Latency & Usage Frequency Observation**
* **Visual Focus:** Stopwatch timing paired with continuous outlet temperature monitoring under two operating conditions:
  * *Condition A (Long Non-Use):* Water left stagnant in lines overnight requires **5 minutes** of purging to exhaust dead-leg cooling and reach steady-state discharge temperature (>45 °C).
  * *Condition B (Frequent Use):* Continuous or closely spaced draws achieve full target discharge within **1 minute** due to pre-warmed piping.
* **Narrative Track:** Analyzes thermal inertia and cold-water volume waste within uninsulated CPVC branch lines during extended idle periods.

**Scene 6: Thermodynamic Reference Calculation**
* **Visual Focus:** On-screen graphical overlays illustrating the First Law equation and energy conversion steps:
  * Mass (m): 25 kg
  * Specific Heat (c_p): 4.184 kJ/(kg·K)
  * Delta T (ΔT): 8 K
  * Sensible Heat (Q): 25 × 4.184 × 8 = 836.8 kJ
  * Electrical Equivalence (E): 836.8 / 3600 ≈ 0.232 kWh
* **Narrative Track:** Explains theoretical minimum energy required per 8 °C elevation batch versus measured active draw time (44 minutes full reheat cycle from ambient 25 °C to 65 °C).

**Scene 7: Loss Mechanisms & Conservation Strategies**
* **Visual Focus:** Thermal dissipation pathways (jacket skin dissipation and uninsulated pipe losses).
* **Narrative Track:** Presents engineering interventions:
  * Installation of automated 6-hour night-time power isolation relays (saving up to 25% standby loss).
  * CPVC line thermal lagging.
  * Seasonal thermostat setpoint adjustment from 65 °C down to 50 °C.

## 3. Ethics, Privacy & Compliance Guidelines
To maintain strict institutional compliance and personal privacy standards during filming:

* **Zero Student Visibility:** Frame all camera angles strictly on equipment, walls, piping, and measurement instruments. No students, staff, or bystanders may appear in any footage.
* **Exclusion of Personal Data:** Avoid recording hostel room numbers, personal belongings, laundry, name boards, or institutional administrative notices.
* **Controlled Access:** Conduct all filming exclusively during authorized maintenance hours under facility escort.
