# Observation and Documentary Plan

1. Introduce the seven-hostel distributed system.
2. Show representative heater installations.
3. Show permitted nameplate/specification evidence.
4. Demonstrate temperature measurement.
5. Explain 37 °C normal water and >45 °C heated outlet observation.
6. Explain the 5-minute long-non-use and 1-minute frequent-use observations.
7. Explain the 25 L/2 kW reference calculation.
8. Discuss heat losses and energy-saving recommendations.

Avoid recording students, personal information or private documents.
