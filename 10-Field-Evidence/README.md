## 3. Observational Summary Notes
* **Water Quality:** Normal municipal tap supply with minimal scale build-up observed inside lower heating element mounts.
* **Safety Valves:** Pressure relief valves (PRVs) were functional across tested units with zero active leakage detected.
* **Electrical Supply:** Stable 230 V AC single-phase power delivered to all individual wall sockets.
