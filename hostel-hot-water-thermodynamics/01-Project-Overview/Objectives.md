# Objectives

- Analyse the hostel heater as a thermodynamic system.
- Apply the Zeroth Law, First Law and SFEE.
- Use measured water temperatures in calculations.
- Quantify reference sensible heat for a 25 L storage volume.
- Compare hostel-wise heater counts.
- Identify heat-transfer modes and losses.
- Develop a basis for future measured efficiency calculations.
- Recommend practical energy-saving improvements.
