# Graphs

These graphs use only available field observations and clearly stated reference calculations.

- `Heater-Distribution-by-Hostel.png` — actual heater counts across the seven hostels.
- `Temperature-Observation.png` — 37 °C observed normal water and 45 °C minimum reference for the >45 °C heated observation.
- `Availability-Time-Observation.png` — approximately 5 min after prolonged non-use and approximately 1 min during frequent use.
- `Reference-Useful-Heat.png` — 0.232 kWh reference sensible heat for 25 kg water from 37 °C to 45 °C.

A true temperature-vs-time graph should be created only after collecting exact timed temperature measurements.
